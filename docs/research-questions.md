# Research Questions and Hypotheses

## Research questions

- **RQ1:** How robust are camera-derived hand landmarks and motion features under tremor-like motion and realistic recording variation?
- **RQ2:** How do frame/feature baselines compare with temporal models for classifying intended gesture sequences and non-command intervals?
- **RQ3:** Can confidence and temporal-persistence gating reduce unintended directional command events, and what latency/rejection trade-off does this introduce?
- **RQ4:** How well do results generalize to participants and recording sessions excluded from training?

## Hypotheses to preregister (only after protocol finalization)

- **H1:** Temporal models may improve sequence-level recognition over a frame-only baseline under the same participant-wise split.
- **H2:** A conservative reject gate may reduce false directional activations while increasing rejected or delayed commands.
- **H3:** Random-window evaluation may overestimate generalization compared with participant-wise evaluation.

These are hypotheses, not findings. Define primary outcomes, thresholds, sample size rationale, and analysis before examining the final test set.
