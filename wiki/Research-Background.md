# Research Background

Hand gestures offer a potential non-contact interface for assistive technologies. Vision-based approaches estimate hand pose or motion from camera frames, while temporal models can represent how gestures evolve over time.

In wheelchair interaction, the system must distinguish an intended command from uncertain motion. Involuntary movement may complicate this task, and conventional accuracy alone does not capture the consequences of false directional commands.

The proposed study is limited to RGB-camera input. Sensor-based tremor datasets and generic gesture datasets must be treated according to their actual modalities and labels.
