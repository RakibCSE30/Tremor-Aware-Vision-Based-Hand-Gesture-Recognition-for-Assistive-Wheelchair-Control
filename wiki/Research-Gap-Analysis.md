# Research Gap Analysis

These are candidate gaps requiring systematic literature validation:

1. **Intent versus movement:** detecting a hand pose or tremor-like movement does not necessarily identify the user's intended wheelchair command.
2. **Temporal ambiguity:** intentional gesture dynamics and involuntary motion may overlap; fixed filtering can remove useful motion or fail to resolve ambiguity.
3. **Data alignment:** synchronized RGB video, intended-command labels, non-command intervals, and participant metadata may be unavailable in existing datasets.
4. **Safety metrics:** false directional activations, missed commands, false stops, rejection, and latency deserve explicit evaluation beyond aggregate accuracy.

For each gap, document supporting and contradicting papers, task/modality differences, and what remains genuinely unresolved.
