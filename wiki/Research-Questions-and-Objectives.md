# Research Questions and Objectives

## Questions

- How robust are camera-derived hand landmarks and motion features under tremor-like motion?
- How do temporal models compare with interpretable baselines?
- Can confidence and persistence gating reduce false directional command events?
- How well do models generalize to unseen participants and sessions?

## Objectives

Audit data, define labels and protocol, implement reproducible baselines, compare temporal models, evaluate command gating, and report safety-oriented errors and runtime performance.
